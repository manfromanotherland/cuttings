# Product Name Research

Checked **2026-08-23**. This is a knockout screen for a local-first macOS app and browser
extension that save articles, images, video, and selected text as visual cards while retaining
their origin.

**Decision, 2026-08-24:** the product name is **Cuttings**.

This is not legal clearance. An exact-name search can find obvious conflicts, but it does not
cover confusingly similar names, unregistered rights, every country, or every store. The
[USPTO's own guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching)
says a comprehensive clearance search involves more than its federal database.

## Decision

Use **Cuttings** as the product name.

It has the best combination of product meaning and observed ownability in this shortlist:

- A cutting is a piece taken from a larger source, so the name naturally covers articles,
  quotes, images, and video clips while implying that the source still matters.
- The plural reads as a personal collection rather than a generic software category.
- No exact adjacent knowledge, bookmark, read-later, or media-library product surfaced in the
  targeted scan.
- Apple's public US catalog returned no app title beginning with `Cuttings`, no exact Chrome
  extension surfaced, the exact npm package returned no public package, and the exact GitHub
  username returned no public account.
- The only exact live US federal word mark surfaced by the knockout search is for wine, not
  software.

The cost is domain strategy. `cuttings.com` is registered and uses Afternic nameservers;
`cuttings.app` is also registered but currently has name servers associated with failed contact
verification. The Google Registry RDAP endpoint returned no domain object for `cuttings.dev`,
which is consistent with it being unregistered at the time checked, but an RDAP 404 is not a
reservation or a guarantee that a registrar will accept an order.

### Recommendation and backups

1. **Cuttings** — lead name; strongest semantic fit and cleanest exact collision screen.
2. **Rerum** — backup only; distinctive in consumer knowledge software, but already used by a
   browser extension that captures a product's name, image, price, and source link into an app,
   as well as other exact-name technology products.
3. **Wayside** — backup only; evocative, but exact active travel and HR software make search and
   brand discovery busier.

Do not carry the other checked names into the next round. **Offcuts** has an active exact-name
Productivity app on iPad, Mac, and Windows. **Findings** has a discontinued but directly adjacent
Mac/iOS research-library app. **Stillroom** has active exact-name writing and private-journal
products. **Acervo**, **Commonplace**, **Kept**, and **Whence** have still stronger product or
trademark conflicts described below. **Cairn** and **Pile** were removed before this round: Cairn
is already used by multiple local-first notes/knowledge tools, and Pile is an exact-category app
for private, local/iCloud-backed notes, bookmarks, files, images, and articles.

## Why `mymind` cannot be the product name

`mymind` is not merely a registered domain or a similar idea. It is an active exact-category
incumbent:

- The [official product site](https://mymind.com/) describes one private visual place for notes,
  bookmarks, articles, and images. Its [product explanation](https://mymind.com/what) calls saved
  items “cards” and says its extension saves an image, text highlight, or website in one click.
- Its [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/mymind-%E2%80%94-an-extension-for/nmgcefdhjpjefhgcpocffdlibknajbmj)
  explicitly offers right-click saving for selected text and images and one-click saving for pages
  and articles. It showed 100,000 users and an update date of 2026-06-03 when checked.
- Apple lists active [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mymind-extend-your-mind/id1520332347)
  and [macOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mymind-extend-your-mind/id1532801185?mt=12)
  products by mymind, Inc. The iOS listing includes images, notes, articles, text highlights,
  bookmarks, cards, media, and returning to the original source.
- MyMind Inc. owns a live US registration for the exact standard-character mark `MYMIND`:
  [serial 98326508, registration 7,564,804](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/summary?serial=98326508).
  Its class 042 services expressly cover software for saving and remembering bookmarks, notes,
  quotes, articles, images, links, and online information.
- [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/mymind.com) shows `mymind.com`
  registered since 2002-06-20 and currently expiring 2030-06-20.

This is the closest possible product, capture interaction, vocabulary, and federal mark. `mymind`
is therefore a hard rejection, not a negotiable naming inconvenience.

## Crowded category language

The obvious category words are weak places to search for a distinctive name:

- **Mind** is dominated by the exact incumbent above and is heavily descriptive across knowledge
  and wellness software.
- **Memory** is similarly descriptive. [Mem](https://help.mem.ai/) already captures text, links,
  files, ideas, meetings, and research through a Mac app and Chrome extension.
- **Recall** is a direct knowledge-product name. [Recall](https://www.recall.it/) saves articles,
  videos, podcasts, PDFs, and notes in one click through browser extensions and mobile apps.
- **Stash** is crowded by an exact [read-later/bookmark product](https://stash.link/), an active
  [save-posts app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stash-save-posts-watch-later/id6761720583), and
  a separate local [macOS clipboard app](https://stash.hexul.com/).
- **Archive** is descriptive and already central to
  [ArchiveBox](https://archivebox.io/), which saves sites, bookmarks, media, and research into
  durable local files using a browser extension, CLI, and self-hosted interface.
- **Muse** has both dense software use and recent trademark history. The local-first visual canvas
  formerly named Muse became Allume after its creator sold the Muse name and trademarks, according
  to the [official rename FAQ](https://allume.com/allume-faq/).

The practical naming lesson is to prefer an ownable object or material metaphor over a generic
promise about mind, memory, recall, storing, or archiving. `Cuttings` follows that direction while
still explaining what the library contains.

## Names excluded before the shortlist

- **Pile** is already the same category. [PILE](https://pile.ie/) describes a private Mac/iPhone/
  iPad collection of notes, bookmarks, files, images, articles, and documents stored in iCloud and
  accessible through Finder. Apple also lists a current
  [Pile read-later app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pile/id6767336093) with a share sheet,
  extension, private iCloud sync, offline articles, tags, highlights, and search.
- **Cairn** has several exact local-first knowledge uses, including
  [a local-first notes app](https://www.trycairn.io/),
  [a Markdown/YAML project-and-notes app](https://ddutchie.github.io/cairn-site/docs/index.html),
  and [a desktop environment-knowledge app](https://cairnapp.dev/). Even before trademark work,
  the exact product namespace is too crowded.

## What remains before launching `Cuttings`

1. Reserve the preferred product domain and social/store namespaces; do not announce first.
2. Check the name inside App Store Connect. Public Apple search is useful evidence but does not
   reserve an app name.
3. Run a professional confusingly-similar search in the intended launch countries, not only an
   exact US word-mark search. WIPO itself says its
   [Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) does not replace
   national and regional register searches. EUIPO/WIPO dynamic result sets were not independently
   verified in this pass.
4. Test the name aloud and in copy: “Save to Cuttings,” “Open Cuttings,” “a cutting from…,” and
   whether users read it as editorial clippings rather than garden propagation.
5. Re-run the store, registry, and trademark checks immediately before filing or launch; all of
   these surfaces can change.
