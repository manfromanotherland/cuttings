Read Later is a distraction-free reading app that keeps your saved articles in a folder you control — on iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or any directory you like. No accounts, no servers, just files.

## Saving Articles

Install the [browser extension](https://read-later.app/extension) and click its toolbar button to save any page. The article is cleaned up, converted to Markdown, and added to your library instantly. The app watches your library folder and picks up new articles the moment they arrive.

## Your Reading List

The **sidebar** on the left organizes your library into smart views:

- **All** — every article that is not archived
- **Unread** — articles waiting to be read
- **Read** — articles you have finished
- **Archive** — articles you have set aside
- **Favorites** — articles you have starred with ♥

Below the smart views you will find sections for **Tags** and **Star Ratings**, so you can filter your library by topic or quality in one click.

## Reading an Article

Click any article in the list to open it in the reader. Press **↑** and **↓** to move between articles without reaching for the mouse.

## Highlights

Select any passage of text in the reader and right-click to choose **Highlight**. Your highlights appear in the **Highlights inspector** — open it with the toolbar button or press **⌘⇧H**. Highlights are saved as plain files in your library, so they are readable and searchable even outside the app.

## Search

Press **⌘K** to jump to the search field. Search scans titles, excerpts, and full article text at once, ranking results by relevance. While searching, you can still switch to any sort order — by date saved, time to read, or rating.

## Tags

While an article is open, press **⌘⇧T** to open the tag picker and add tags. Tags appear in the sidebar as live filters, making it easy to group articles by project, topic, or anything that makes sense to you.

## Ratings

Click the star icons in the toolbar to rate the open article. Your rated articles appear under **Star Ratings** in the sidebar, making it easy to filter to the best things you have saved.

## Appearance

Open **Preferences → Typography** to choose your font family, size, and line width. Switch between light and dark mode under **Preferences → Appearance**.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Press **⌘/** to open the keyboard-shortcuts cheat sheet. Every action in Read Later has a shortcut so you can move through your library without lifting your hands from the keyboard.
