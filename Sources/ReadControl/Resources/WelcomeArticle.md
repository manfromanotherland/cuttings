ReadControl lets you manage your readings easily. Everything you save lives in a plain folder you control — on iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, or any directory you like. No accounts, no servers, just files.

Here is a quick tour to get you started.

## Saving readings

Install the [browser extension](https://readcontrol.app/extension) and click its toolbar button to save any page. The extension strips out all the clutter — the modals, the banners, the ads — and saves what is left to your library as Markdown. The app watches your library folder and picks up new readings the moment they arrive.

## Your reading list

The **sidebar** on the left organizes your library into smart views:

- **All** — every reading that is not archived
- **Unread** — readings waiting for you
- **Read** — readings you have finished
- **Archive** — readings you have set aside
- **Favorites** — readings you have marked with ♥

Below the smart views you will find **Tags** and **Ratings**, so you can narrow your library by topic or quality in a single click.

## Reading

Click any reading in the list to open it in the reader. Press **↑** and **↓** to move between readings without reaching for the mouse.

## Highlights

Select any passage in the reader and right-click to choose **Highlight**. Your highlights collect in the **Highlights inspector** — open it from the toolbar or press **⌘⇧H**. Like everything else, highlights are saved as plain files in your library, so they stay readable and searchable even outside the app.

## Search

Press **⌘K** to jump to the search field. Search scans titles, excerpts, and full text at once and ranks results by relevance. You can still switch to any sort order while searching — by date saved, time to read, or rating.

## Tags

With a reading open, press **⌘⇧T** to add tags. Tags show up in the sidebar as live filters, so you can group readings by project, topic, or anything that makes sense to you.

## Ratings

Click the stars in the toolbar to rate whatever you are reading — and don't forget to rate them at the end. Rated readings appear under **Ratings** in the sidebar, making it easy to find the best things you have saved.

## Typography and appearance

Open **Settings → Typography** to set the font type and size, along with the width of the text column, so everything you read looks the way you want it to. Switch between light and dark mode under **Settings → Appearance**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **⌘/** for the shortcuts cheat sheet. Every action in ReadControl has a shortcut, so you can move through your library without lifting your hands from the keyboard.

---

Every page you save lives on your machine as a plain `article.md` file. You own them, and you can move them whenever you want. I would keep them in a folder that syncs to the cloud too — Dropbox, iCloud, Google Drive, whichever you already pay for.

Enjoy your readings!
