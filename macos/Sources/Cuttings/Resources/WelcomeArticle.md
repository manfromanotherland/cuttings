Cuttings helps you save articles, images, videos, and quotes from the web and return to them whenever you want. Everything you save lives in a plain folder you control, on iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, or wherever you like. No accounts, no servers, just files.

Here is a quick tour to get you started.

## Saving from the web

Install the Cuttings browser extension from this project. Use its toolbar button to save a full article, or right-click an image, video, or text selection and choose **Add to Cuttings**. Every card keeps the page it came from, and articles are cleaned into readable Markdown before they reach your library.

## Your library

The list on the left keeps everything tidy:

- **All**, everything you have saved that is not archived
- **Unread**, what is still waiting for you
- **Read**, what you have finished
- **Archive**, what you have set aside
- **Favorites**, the ones you loved

Below those, **Tags** and **Ratings** let you narrow things down by topic or quality in a click.

## Reading

Click any article in the list to open it. Press **↑** and **↓** to move from one to the next without reaching for the mouse.

## Highlights

Select any text while you read, right-click, and choose **Highlight**. Everything you highlight gathers in one place, which you can open from the toolbar or with **⌘⇧H**. Your highlights are saved right next to the article, so they stay with you even outside the app.

## Search

Press **⌘K** to search. It looks through everything you have saved and puts the closest matches first. You can still sort while you search, by date, time to read, or rating.

## Tags

With an article open, press **⌘⇧T** to add tags. Your tags appear on the left, so you can group things by project, topic, or anything that makes sense to you.

## Ratings

Click the stars in the toolbar to rate what you are reading, and remember to give it a score at the end. Anything you rate shows up under **Ratings**, so the best of what you saved is always easy to find.

## Typography and appearance

Open **Settings → Typography** to pick the font and size, and how wide the text sits on the page, so everything looks the way you like. Switch between light and dark under **Settings → Appearance**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **⌘/** to see every shortcut. Just about everything in Cuttings has one, so you can fly through without lifting your hands from the keyboard.

Everything you save stays on your computer as a simple file that belongs to you. You can move it, back it up, or open it anywhere. I would keep it all in a folder that syncs to the cloud, like Dropbox, iCloud, or Google Drive, whichever you already use.
