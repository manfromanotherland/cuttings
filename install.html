Get the ReadControl app

To save pages to your library, you need the free ReadControl app on your Mac. The extension hands each page to the app, which saves it as a file you own.

Download ReadControl →
1
Download and install the app
Get ReadControl from readcontrol.app and move it to your Applications folder.
2
Open it once
Launch ReadControl and choose the folder where your saved pages will live. It connects to this extension automatically.
3
Save any page
Click the ReadControl toolbar button (or press ⌃⇧S) on any page you want to keep.

Already installed it? Make sure you've opened the ReadControl app at least once, then try saving again. If you moved the app after opening it, open it once more so it can reconnect.
